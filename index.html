2D skeletal animation from a single picture.

Drop a character image or a layered PSD. RABBIT cuts it out, places the skeleton, builds meshes and weights, and gives you walk, run, jump and attack in seconds — then export to Spine 4.2 or play it in your engine.

Open Studio Try the player

Built for small teams shipping 2D games

AI rigging

  • Background removal and a mesh fitted to the silhouette
  • Joints from the silhouette, layer names, or a vision model
  • Humanoid, quadruped and blob templates; side or front view
  • Hair, cloth and tails get physics automatically

Spine compatible

  • Imports Spine 4.2 JSON: meshes, IK, path, transform and physics constraints, clipping, sequences
  • Exports Spine 4.2 JSON + atlas, plus GIF
  • Matches the official runtime bone-for-bone on 99 of 105 example animations

One core, every engine

  • Rust core: WebAssembly + WebGL2 on the web
  • Godot 4.4+ (GDExtension)
  • Unity 2021.3+ (Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS)
  • Same animation, same physics, everywhere

How it works

  1. DropA PNG of one character on a plain background, or a layered PSD for the best result.
  2. Check the jointsDrag any dot that's off. Add bones, paint weights where needed.
  3. Pick motionsWalk, run, jump, attack, hit, die — retargeted to your rig, editable on a timeline.
  4. ExportSpine 4.2 JSON + atlas, a GIF, or load it straight into the Web, Godot or Unity runtime.
